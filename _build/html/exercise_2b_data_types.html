"There are 556 cats and monitor lizards in Lumpini park."

They want to use Python to calculate the number (556) in the message, using the number_of_monitor_lizards and number_of_cats variables (e.g. they do NOT want to hard-code the number in the message text).

At the moment they are not getting the result they want…can you fix their code?
